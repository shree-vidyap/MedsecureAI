Welcome to MedSecure

Fake and expired medicines are hard to spot by eye. MedSecure AI helps you check a pack in seconds, using the codes and text already printed on it, and gives you a clear report you can save or download.

  • Reads QR codes and barcodes
  • Validates the barcode check digit
  • Compares the QR with the printed batch
  • Warns you about expired medicine

What MedSecure checks for you

QR and barcode check

Validates the barcode number and reads the data inside the QR code.

Batch and expiry match

Compares the QR with the printed batch and expiry, and flags expired packs.

AI print inspection

A trained model looks for blurred, low quality or tampered printing.

MedSecure AI is an assistive verification tool. It does not replace professional or pharmacist verification.
