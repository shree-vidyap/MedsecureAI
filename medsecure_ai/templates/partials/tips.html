STAY SAFE - MEDICINE SAFETY TIPS

Scan before you take

Check the pack with MedSecure AI first.

Check the expiry date

Never use a medicine after its expiry date.

Buy from licensed shops

Use trusted, licensed pharmacies only.

Look at the packaging

Broken seal, blurry print or spelling mistakes are warning signs.

Store it properly

Keep in original pack, in a cool dry place, away from children.

Ask if unsure

Talk to your doctor or pharmacist when in doubt.
